## Market read (Trend Researcher)

**The opportunity:** there's a large, underserved gap between "intimidated by
CrossFit" and "ready to walk into a box." Most CrossFit marketing targets people
who are *already* fit or competitive. Beginners 25+ — especially busy
professionals in downtown Chicago and NYC — are curious but scared off by:

- Fear of injury and lifting with bad form in a fast group class
- "I need to get in shape *before* I start" (the readiness trap)
- Intimidating box culture, Rx weights, leaderboard/competitive vibe
- Being lost and embarrassed in a class moving too fast for a newcomer

**The angle you can own:** *the beginner-safe, coached on-ramp to CrossFit-style
strength.* Not a box. Not a bootcamp. A coach who builds the foundation 1:1 (and
in small beginner groups) so people get strong safely and actually stick. Your
premium, individualized model is the antidote to the "thrown in the deep end"
fear — and downtown professionals with disposable income will pay for it.

Three angles, ranked:
1. **"CrossFit without the intimidation"** — beginner-first, strength-led, coached. *(Recommended)*
2. **"Get strong for real life, after 25"** — capability/longevity for busy adults.
3. **"Done-for-you strength programming"** — for the self-directed who want a plan.

→ Lead with #1; use #2 and #3 as supporting messages.

## Positioning statement (Brand Guardian)

> **I help adults 25+ in Chicago and NYC who are curious about CrossFit but
> intimidated by it get genuinely strong — through 1:1 coaching and individualized
> programming that builds the foundation first, so they progress safely and
> actually stick with it.**

---

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

- **Who:** Professionals 25–45, downtown Chicago / NYC, desk-bound, disposable income.
- **Goals:** Get strong, look capable, feel confident; "I want to try CrossFit but
  not get hurt or embarrassed."
- **Pains:** Intimidated by boxes; tried a class and felt lost; afraid of injury;
  no plan; time-poor; bored of generic gym routines.
- **Objections:** Price ("$140 is a lot"); time ("I'm too busy"); readiness ("I'm
  not fit enough yet"); "can't I just join a box for $200/mo?".
- **Where they are:** Instagram/TikTok (fitness + "beginner CrossFit" content),
  Google ("personal trainer downtown Chicago", "CrossFit for beginners NYC"),
  referrals from current clients, downtown coworking/office networks.

## Message pillars (repeat these everywhere)

1. **Strength first, not chaos.** Beginner-safe foundations before intensity.
2. **You won't be thrown in the deep end.** Coached 1:1, scaled to you, no Rx pressure, no judgment.
3. **Built for adults 25+.** Real-life capability and longevity, not leaderboard scores.
4. **A plan, not a guess.** Individualized programming so every session has a purpose.
5. **Curious about CrossFit? Start here.** The on-ramp that makes the box feel doable.
